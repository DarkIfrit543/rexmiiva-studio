Riseveil

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

Riseveil is a focus and productivity application developed by Rexmiiva Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Riseveil and how that information is used.

2. Information stored on your device

Riseveil stores your Journey progress locally on your device. This may include your hero configuration, focus sessions, progression, rewards, settings, and other information required for the app to function.

Riseveil does not currently provide cloud synchronization for your Journey progress. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored progress.

3. Google account connection

Riseveil allows you to optionally connect a Google account. Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication are used to authenticate your account.

When you connect a Google account, information associated with the authenticated account may be processed, including your account identifier, display name, and email address.

Connecting a Google account does not currently upload or synchronize your Riseveil Journey progress.

4. Authentication credentials

Authentication is handled using Google identity services and Firebase Authentication. Riseveil does not store your Google password.

Authentication tokens are used as necessary to establish and maintain your authenticated session. Riseveil does not intentionally log or store Google ID tokens as part of your Journey data.

5. Purchases

Riseveil may offer a one-time purchase through Google Play. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Rexmiiva Studio does not directly collect or store your payment card or bank account information.

Information provided by Google Play about purchase status may be processed as necessary to determine access to purchased features.

6. Analytics and advertising

Riseveil does not currently use third-party analytics or crash reporting services.

Riseveil does not currently display advertising or include an advertising SDK.

7. Notifications and focus features

Riseveil may use Android system capabilities for notifications, alarms, focus-related features, and restoring relevant app state after the device restarts. These capabilities are used to provide the app's focus functionality and are not used for advertising.

8. Third-party services

Riseveil uses services provided by Google, including Google identity services, Firebase Authentication, and Google Play Billing. These services may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data retention, disconnection and account deletion

Journey data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed through the app's available reset functionality, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, depending on the type of data involved.

You can disconnect your connected Google account from Riseveil. Disconnecting the account signs the app out of its authentication session but does not delete locally stored Journey progress.

Deleting your Riseveil account is a separate action that removes the Firebase Authentication identity and its linked Google profile information, not your Google Account. In versions with deletion controls, use Profile → Settings → Google account → Delete Riseveil account. Recent Google verification may be required. Local Journey progress, Veil Shards and cosmetics remain on this device; Google Play purchases are managed separately. Connecting again creates a new Riseveil account.

You can also request Riseveil account deletion by email without installing the app. That page explains ownership verification, affected data and retention limitations. Disconnect and Journey Reset do not delete the Firebase account.

10. Children's privacy

Riseveil is not specifically designed or directed toward children. Rexmiiva Studio does not knowingly use Riseveil to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Riseveil's features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices related to Riseveil, please contact Rexmiiva Studio through the support information provided on this website.
